# SAARTHI – Privacy Policy

**Organisation:** Saarthi Counsel  
**App:** SAARTHI  
**Last updated:** 10 November 2026

## 1. Introduction

Saarthi Counsel ("we", "our" or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the SAARTHI app collects, uses and handles personal information when you submit a student registration or counselling enquiry.

## 2. Information We Collect

When you submit the registration form, we may collect:

- Full name
- Mobile number
- Email address, if provided
- Student status and stream
- Selected service and package
- Preferred course, if provided
- Counselling mode
- City and district
- Additional information voluntarily provided by you
- Your consent to be contacted regarding your enquiry

We also process your date of birth to check that you are at least 18 years old. The date of birth is used for this eligibility check and is not included in the student registration record.

For registration abuse prevention, our server also processes a hashed representation of the request's IP address.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to:

- Process student registrations and counselling enquiries.
- Contact students regarding their enquiries.
- Understand students' educational interests and service requirements.
- Manage registrations and follow-ups.
- Prevent misuse of the registration service.
- Operate and improve the SAARTHI service.

## 4. Data Storage and Service Providers

Registration information is stored in our Supabase-powered backend and accessed through our authorised administration system.

Supabase provides database and backend infrastructure for the app. Information may be processed by service providers involved in operating this infrastructure.

## 5. Data Sharing

We do not intend to sell students' personal information. Information may be accessed by authorised personnel or service providers where necessary to operate the service, respond to enquiries, maintain the system or comply with applicable legal requirements.

## 6. Data Security

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect registration information against unauthorised access, disclosure or misuse.

However, no internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 7. Age Eligibility

SAARTHI registration is currently restricted to individuals aged 18 or older. The registration system checks the date of birth before accepting a registration.
